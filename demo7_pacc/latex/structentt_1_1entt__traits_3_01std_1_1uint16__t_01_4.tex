\doxysubsection{详细描述}
Entity traits for a 16 bits entity identifier. 

A 16 bits entity identifier guarantees\+:


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item 12 bits for the entity number (up to 4k entities).
\item 4 bit for the version (resets in \mbox{[}0-\/15\mbox{]}). 
\end{DoxyItemize}
